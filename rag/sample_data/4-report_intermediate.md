Introduction
Cerulean Shield Cyber Assurance LLP conducted a VAPT engagement for Harborline 
Freight Technologies Pvt. Ltd. covering the RouteLedger Transport Management System 
web portal used by operations, branch dispatch, finance operations and claims teams.
Objective
Assess the application security posture, validate access-control enforcement, identify 
exploitable web application weaknesses, and provide evidence-backed remediation 
guidance suitable for engineering and governance tracking.
Business Context
RouteLedger supports branch shipment planning, proof-of-delivery handling, claims intake, 
finance reconciliation and exception management. Findings were prioritized based on 
exposure of shipment records, finance exports, operational disruption and account 
takeover risk.
Limitations / Assumptions

Testing was limited to authorized UAT endpoints and roles provided by Harborline; production systems, third-party payment 
providers and live customer notification channels were excluded.

No destructive test cases, denial-of-service tests or bulk data extraction activities were performed.

Credentials were provided for representative roles. Findings may not cover permissions for unprovided roles or newly deployed 
features after the assessment window.

Evidence screenshots were captured using auditor workstations with tokens and business data redacted before inclusion in this 
report.
Management Summary
The most material risk is inconsistent authorization enforcement across shipment documents and finance export functions. Input 
validation weaknesses also increase the risk of cross-site scripting, application error disclosure and unsafe file handling.
